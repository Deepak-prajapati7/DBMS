What We Accept

14 electronic waste streams. Handled with care.

Whether you have an old smartphone, a swollen laptop battery, a cracked television, or commercial server hardware, our authorized recyclers ensure safe containment and maximum component reuse.

Handhelds

Smartphones & Tablets

Android, iPhones & iPads. Free factory reset & safe lithium battery handling.

Tip: Remove SIM and microSD cards before handover.
Computers

Laptops & Notebooks

Working or non-functional. High-yield salvage for RAM, SSDs, and aluminum enclosures.

Tip: Include charging brick if available for testing.
Hazardous Care

Batteries (Lithium & Lead)

Power banks, phone batteries & UPS cells. Transported in fire-safe thermal sleeves.

Tip: Tape exposed metallic terminals with insulation tape.
Displays

Televisions & Monitors

CRT, LED & OLED panels. Specialized phosphorus, circuit, and glass recycling.

Tip: Keep front glass intact to prevent sharp hazards.
Telecom

Networking Gear

Wi-Fi routers, network switches, modems, patch panels & optical receivers.

Tip: Reset network admin credentials to default before collection.
Peripherals

Printers & Scanners

Desktop inkjets, laser printers & flatbed scanners. Plastic and motor recovery.

Tip: Remove ink or toner cartridges to avoid leaks.
Wearables

Smartwatches & Audio Bands

Smartwatches, fitness bands, wireless earbuds & charging cases with micro-cells.

Tip: Disconnect Bluetooth sync from your mobile device.
Entertainment

Gaming Consoles

PlayStation, Xbox & Nintendo hardware. Tested for HDMI, power supply, and drive salvage.

Tip: Eject any game media and co-package gamepads.
High Yield

PC Hardware & GPUs

Motherboards, graphics cards, CPUs, heatsinks & desktop computer enclosures.

Tip: Highest yield for reclaimed copper, aluminum, and gold.
Audio

Audio & Studio Equipment

Headphones, home theater speakers, amplifiers, subwoofers & mixing boards.

Tip: Bundle audio jacks and external power cords together.
Accessories

Cables & Chargers

USB cords, HDMI leads, power adapters & extension power strips. 100% copper reclaim.

Tip: Bundle cords loosely with a tie or rubber band.
Data Security

Storage (HDDs & SSDs)

Internal SATA hard drives, NVMe chips & external backup drives. Multi-pass data sanitization.

Tip: Certified cryptographic wiping or physical shredding.
Enterprise

Server Equipment & Racks

1U/2U rack servers, SAN storage arrays & enterprise data center power units.

Tip: Bulk manifest uploads and Form-6 certificates provided.
Power

Power Supplies & SMPS

Computer power supplies, inverter boards & uninterruptible power supplies (UPS).

Tip: Wrap exposed wiring leads before pickup handover.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is doorstep pickup free for households and individual citizens? ↓

Yes, doorstep collection is 100% free for individual citizens and households across supported PIN codes. Our operational costs are funded through material recovery yield and the circular parts marketplace.

What happens to my personal data on discarded laptops and phones? ↓

We recommend performing a factory reset before handing over any device. At our collection hub, all storage drives undergo multi-pass cryptographic data wiping (DoD 5220.22-M compliant). Drives that cannot be wiped are physically shredded.

Do you accept completely broken, rusted, or dead electronics? ↓

Absolutely. Even non-functional or shattered electronics contain valuable recyclable metals—such as copper, aluminum, and trace gold—as well as toxic substances (lead, mercury) that must be kept out of landfills.

How are salvaged parts verified before listing on the marketplace? ↓

Every salvaged component undergoes rigorous diagnostic testing: memory modules run through MemTest86, storage drives are verified with SMART health logs, and power supplies undergo multimeter voltage stability tests before receiving a "Tested" badge.

How do payments and refunds work on the spare parts marketplace? ↓

All payments are securely handled through Razorpay via UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, or Netbanking. Every order comes with an instant downloadable receipt and is protected by a 14-day return and test guarantee.

Can universities, schools, or corporate offices schedule bulk pickups? ↓

Yes. Corporate and institutional customers can schedule bulk pallet pickups with batch CSV manifests. EcoCycle provides official Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Form-6 green recycling certificates for ESG compliance.

    Recycling procedure

    1. Prepare the device

      Back up your files, sign out of accounts, and erase personal data where possible. Remove SIM and memory cards.

    2. Keep items safe

      Keep devices dry and intact. Do not open electronics or remove built-in batteries. Follow the recycler’s instructions for loose batteries.

    3. Arrange the handover

      Submit a pickup or drop-off request. Confirm that the location accepts your items before you travel.

    4. Use authorised processing

      Items should be assessed for reuse or repair, then processed by an appropriate recycler that recovers materials and manages residues.

    Battery safety. If a battery is swollen, hot, leaking or damaged, do not handle or transport it yourself. Contact the collection provider for instructions. Local regulations and recycler requirements take precedence.
